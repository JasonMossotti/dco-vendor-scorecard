Telemetry
One-minute samples: GPUs in the published format of NVIDIA's open-source DCGM exporter, CDUs in the DMTF Redfish schema for cooling units, power in the manufacturers' published SNMP and Modbus point maps, the network under UFM Telemetry, node_exporter, and NMX-T names, chillers and air handlers over BACnet/IP, the management network and storage over Redfish and SNMPv3.
