Each finding's action:

- **Past its grace window with nothing recorded.** The Landlord books it within 2 business days and the Customer's account lead is told; the device shows PM overdue until the work is recorded, or a Customer-signed deferral sets a new date.
- **Booked after its grace window.** The Landlord moves the booking inside the grace window through the change board, or asks for a Customer-signed deferral with the reason, the risk, and a new date.
- **Due before the horizon with nothing booked.** The Customer sends the Landlord a booking request; the Landlord books it inside the due window within 5 business days.
- **Needs PM and has no record (no baseline).** The Landlord books a baseline PM for each device within 30 days of the schedule change and records it.

## The coverage procedure (Landlord SLA section 11.1)

Every device the schedule covers has its next task booked on or before its grace limit. The Landlord keeps a PM register by device and task (last completion, next due date, booking), shared read-only with the Customer. Hall C devices join it at handover. Monthly tasks are due in each calendar month and have 7 days of grace; quarterly and semiannual tasks 14 days; annual and longer tasks 30 days.

- **PMC-S1 Weekly coverage check.** Every Monday the Customer runs the coverage check against the register and the maintenance calendar for the next 8 weeks and reviews it at the daily standup.
- **PMC-S2 Due with nothing booked.** The Customer sends the Landlord a booking request; the Landlord books the task inside its due window within 5 business days, through the change board when it takes a unit out of a redundancy group.
- **PMC-S3 Booked late.** The Landlord moves the booking inside the grace window, or asks for a deferral (PMC-S5).
- **PMC-S4 Overdue.** The Landlord books the task within 2 business days and tells the Customer's account lead; the device is shown as PM overdue until the work is recorded with its evidence.
- **PMC-S5 Deferral.** A task may move past its grace window only under a deferral the Customer signs, giving the reason, the risk and how it is covered (for example closer monitoring), and a new date no later than one more interval.
- **PMC-S6 No baseline.** A device the schedule covers with no PM record gets a baseline PM within 30 days of the schedule change or of its handover.

A coverage finding is a planning finding and changes no service level. OT-CSL-06 counts the tasks the Landlord opened; counting a task never booked as missed is a proposal for SLA change control.

## How it works

- **Program.** `config/pm_program.yaml` expands each line of the Landlord SLA's maintenance schedule to the devices it covers (`docs/site/devices.json`), with its interval, grace, and basis. Monthly tasks are due in each calendar month; the rest by days (quarterly 91, semiannual 182, annual 365, three-year 1,095).
- **Last completion.** The latest PM record for the line: the Hall A history for the Hall A CDU filters (the CDU telemetry and the predictions follow it), the July, August, and September Measurement Periods for everything else. Before the records begin, the opening register (`data/pm_coverage/opening.csv`, its own random stream) holds the last completion from the Landlord's maintenance system or the acceptance test at commissioning.
- **Walk.** From the last completion, each due date must have a booking on or before its grace limit; a booking is taken as done on its day and the next due date follows from it, until the horizon.
- **Load bank.** Required only when a monthly exercise did not reach 30% of nameplate (NFPA 110); every recorded exercise did, so it is not required.
- **Synthetic layer.** The opening register is placed by its own planner rule (each line's next due date booked in time, or after the horizon), then one planted record problem of each kind; a line with no records and no date that satisfies the rule is listed as found. The checks never read the answer key. `python scripts/render_pm.py --robustness 60` scores 60 forward schedules and opening registers.
