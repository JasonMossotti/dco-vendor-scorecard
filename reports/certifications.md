## How it works

- **Who must hold what.** Each role's credentials (`config/certifications.yaml` roles) plus the EHS courses the role holds (`config/ehs.yaml`). Work adds its own: an IT Partner incident by category (ESD for tray, optic, and power work, liquid cooling for tray and leak work, fiber for optics, the rack model's FRU authorization for tray and switch trays), a Landlord work order by fault class and a maintenance record by system (UPS, CDU, chiller, generator, VESDA training; EPA 608 for chiller maintenance), a destruction certificate's operator, and every guard post shift (the Texas DPS Level III commission and SA requalification; STG for the posts that draw a shotgun).
- **When.** A shift at its start, when the badge records verify it by the same rule as the staffing service level; an incident when the technician was on site; a work order when engaged; a maintenance record when completed.
- **Reports.** Each partner's monthly credential report is compared with what the register shows lapsed at the month end.
- **KM-15.** The IT Partner's badge-verified shifts on which the person held every role credential; the IT scorecard reads it from here.
- **Synthetic layer.** `data/certs/` is generated on its own random stream with four planted record problems (five answer-key entries); the checks never read the answer key. `python scripts/render_certs.py --robustness 60 --jobs 4` scores 60 generated months.
