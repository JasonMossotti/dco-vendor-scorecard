Each finding's action:

- **Work with no permit.** Customer EHS investigates under the common terms (EHS-R2 or EHS-R3); the partner shows the permit or explains the work, and the permit procedure is briefed before the next job of this kind.
- **Locked out while the device showed power.** Customer EHS investigates: the lockout record and the device disagree on when the circuit was dead. The partner shows the test-before-touch record; the lockout procedure is re-verified on the next job.
- **Named on a permit without current training.** The person stops permit work until the course is current; the partner shows the training record on Site (EHS-C2 if the work went ahead without it, EHS-C3 if only the record was missing).
- **Permit closed before the work ended.** Customer EHS investigates why the permit was closed while the work was still open; the partner re-briefs permit close-out (the job is closed only after the equipment is restored).

## How it works

- **Which jobs need a permit.** From the records (`config/ehs.yaml`): a Landlord MOP or maintenance record by its work type (a tap-off retorque, a UPS maintenance, a CDU filter change, and a chiller maintenance need a lockout; a new tap-off onto live busway needs an energized work permit approved in advance by the Customer and a working-at-height permit), a corrective work order on power or a UPS by its fault class, and the IT Partner's overhead cabling step (milestone M5) by its milestone.
- **The work.** What the device recorded where one records it (the tap-off breaker, the UPS bypass, the CDU pump, the chiller lockout), else the job record.
- **Checks.** A job with no permit of a type it needs, or a tap-off breaker opened with no permit open on it; a lockout recorded outside the device's isolation; a person named on a permit (or an electrical task's engineer) without a current completion of every course the work needs; a permit closed before the work ended.
- **Not findings.** Injuries, near misses, first aid cases, and stop-work uses are reported, counted, and never findings (29 CFR 1904.35).
- **Synthetic layer.** `data/ehs/` is generated on its own random stream with four planted record problems; the checks never read the answer key. `python scripts/render_ehs.py --robustness 60 --jobs 4` scores 60 generated months.
