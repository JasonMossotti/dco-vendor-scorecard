Each finding's action:

- **Two jobs in one redundancy group at once (below N).** Move one of the jobs so the group keeps its redundant unit; the change board checks the group before approving.
- **Work on both feeds of one rack at once.** Move one of the jobs; never book both feeds of a rack in the same window.
- **A unit out on a deployment surge day.** Move the job off the surge day, or move an energization, so new load does not land on a group without its redundant unit.
- **Work inside the first-compute freeze.** Move the job out of the freeze or bring it to the change board as an exception with a rollback plan.
- **Work ran past its booked window.** Record why the work ran long and extend the window through the change board before it closes; review the window length for this kind of work.

## How it works

- **Effect of a job.** Read from its title (`config/calendar.yaml`, work_types): a CDU filter change isolates one of the CDU's two pumps, a UPS maintenance puts the UPS on maintenance bypass, a chiller maintenance locks the chiller out, a tap-off retorque opens one feed of the rack. A new rack's tap-off, a detector test, a generator exercise, and a firmware change on one rack take nothing out of a redundancy group. Where the change layer has an impact declaration, the calendar's effect must equal it.
- **Groups.** The redundancy groups Site health uses (`src/scorecard/site_health.py`), from the site model and the device lists. Each CDU's two pumps are their own group; rack feeds are checked rack by rack.
- **Surge day.** A day with 2 or more Hall B energizations: Interface Agreement HO-4 tap-off energizations and rack power-ons from both partners' Jira (R-HO4, R-M4), done dates for done steps and due dates for the rest.
- **Freeze.** The 7 days before Hall C first compute: no work that takes a unit out of a site-wide group (utility, generators, chillers, facility water pumps) and no Hall C work outside the commissioning plan.
- **Windows.** A MOP or CAB change uses its approved window; a maintenance record without a MOP uses what the device recorded (the chiller's lockout in the BMS, the engine's run in the EMCP record) or the task's length before its recorded completion. A deferred change is shown but is off the calendar for the checks.
- **Forward schedule.** Placed by the planner's own rules (one job at a time in each group, never both feeds of a rack, nothing on a surge day or in the freeze that the rules forbid), then one planted conflict of each kind. The checks never read the answer key. `python scripts/render_calendar.py --robustness 60` scores 60 schedules.
