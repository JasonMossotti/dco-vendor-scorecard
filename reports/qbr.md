# Quarterly business review

Site AUS-1 (synthetic), 2026-Q3: Jul 6 to Sep 27, 2026, three 4-week Measurement Periods. Generated by `scripts/render_qbr.py`; do not edit by hand.

The contract's quarterly forum (`sla/common.yaml`, governance): trend analysis, SLA change proposals, the roadmap and capacity plan, and program-wide lessons learned. The last month is the committed sample; the two before it are generated by the same site generator with their own seeds and their planted problems drawn by chance (`config/qbr.yaml`, `data/quarter/`). September's stories (the rack A07 outage, the Hall B rollout, the audit and EHS cases) are in September only.

The record starts with the quarter: a doubling needs three consecutive defaults inside it, and an earnback, which needs the three months after a default, can only be on track, pending, or lost. A doubling is shown as an adjustment beside the published month; the published scorecard does not change.

Proposal rules (`config/qbr.yaml`): **corrective action plan** when a service level is below Minimum in 2 months or more; **review the measure** when the partner's report met Expected while the measurement did not in every month; **tighten** when Expected was met every month with the worst month beyond Expected by at least 0.5 of the Expected-to-Minimum gap. What each proposal means is the review's judgment, in `qbr/notes/`.

## Program-wide lessons

- Every partner's own report met Expected on service levels the measurement had below it, in every month of the quarter. From October the monthly service review shows the two side by side for each service level.
- Record integrity was below Minimum for the IT Partner in all three months and for the Landlord in two. The same kinds of records keep failing to reconcile, so the corrective plans start with the repeat finding types, not with single findings.
- Once several service levels are chronic, the at-risk cap, not the doubling, decides the amount: the IT Partner's doubled September credits came to $333,000 and the cap held them to $222,000.
